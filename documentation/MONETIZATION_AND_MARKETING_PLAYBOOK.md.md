# CyphalForge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Opportunity

As modern drone and aerospace platforms transition away from legacy UAVCAN v0 / DroneCAN to OpenCyphal (UAVCAN v1.0), avionics OEMs (PX4, ArduPilot, Zubax) face significant toolchain complexity. CyphalForge Pro provides a lightweight, interactive modeling and validation studio with zero compiler setup overhead.

## 2\. Pricing & Commercial Packaging

- **Avionics Developer Seat**: \$99/month (Interactive studio, headless Python CLI, DSDL type validator).  
- **OEM & Flight Lab License**: \$990/month (\$8,500/year perpetual) including custom DSDL schema compiler, CAN FD hardware interface bridges, and dedicated avionics engineering support.
